# Code of conduct

BVT Skills is open to everyone. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be respectful and patient. Critique the work, never the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content.
- No spam, promotion of unrelated projects, or requests to trade stars.
- Keep other people's private information private.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem privately to **support@bitvault.club**.
